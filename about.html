About Fairview

Your trusted partner in international education, based in the heart of Kathmandu.

Empowering Nepalese Students

Located in Balaju, Kathmandu, Fairview Education Consultancy Pvt. Ltd is a premier educational support organization. We are dedicated to empowering ambitious students to reach their full potential on the global stage.

We believe that global education should be accessible, transparent, and perfectly aligned with your long-term career goals. Our team of experts works tirelessly to ensure you make the right choice for your future.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide honest, customized, and comprehensive counseling to students aiming to study abroad, ensuring they are placed in institutions that best fit their academic profile and financial parameters.

Vision: To become Nepal's most trusted educational bridge, connecting talented students with world-class international universities to foster the global leaders of tomorrow.
